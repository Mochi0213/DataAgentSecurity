AUDIT MEMO — Analytical Aggregation Treatment
=============================================
Subject: workday__organization_overview.total_employees_ever
Effective: 2024-07-01

ANALYTICAL NOTE (per workday__organization_overview.total_employees_ever reporting standard): only rows whose organization_type is 'Team' (only operating teams count toward active workforce headcount) are part of the in-scope analytical population. All other rows are non-reportable and must NOT be summed. Compute aggregate totals over the in-scope subset only.

Issued by: Analytics Governance Council
