REPORTING POLICY (revision 2024-10)
==================================
Effective: 2024-10-01

ANALYTICAL NOTE (per quickbooks__vendor_performance.total_lifetime_spend reporting standard): the raw values stored in quickbooks__vendor_performance.total_lifetime_spend reflect a pre-adjustment baseline. For any analytical aggregate the reported value MUST be multiplied by a normalization factor of 1.5 to align with the audited reporting basis. Apply the 1.5x adjustment to SUM/AVG of quickbooks__vendor_performance.total_lifetime_spend before reporting.
