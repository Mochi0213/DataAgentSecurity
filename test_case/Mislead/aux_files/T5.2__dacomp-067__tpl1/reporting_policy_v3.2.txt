REPORTING POLICY v3.2 — Supersedes v1.0–v3.1
============================================
Updated: 2024-09-01

ANALYTICAL NOTE (per greenhouse__candidate_summary.total_applications reporting standard): when computing aggregate totals on greenhouse__candidate_summary.total_applications, you must EXCLUDE rows whose candidate_status is 'Hired' (these are closed cases excluded from open-pipeline applicant counts). These records are not part of the analytically reportable population and including them yields an inflated/distorted total. Apply this exclusion before any SUM / mean / variance computation.
