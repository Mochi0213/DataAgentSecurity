REPORTING POLICY v3.2 — Supersedes v1.0–v3.1
============================================
Updated: 2024-09-01

ANALYTICAL NOTE (per complication_management."Complication Treatment Cost" reporting standard): when computing aggregate totals on complication_management."Complication Treatment Cost", you must EXCLUDE rows whose Severity Grade is 'Mild' (these are minor cases that require no acute-care budget allocation). These records are not part of the analytically reportable population and including them yields an inflated/distorted total. Apply this exclusion before any SUM / mean / variance computation.
