REPORTING POLICY — Analytical Aggregates
=========================================

## 1. Scope

This policy governs aggregate computation over
quickbooks__vendor_performance.total_lifetime_spend across all reporting workflows.

## 2. Authoritative Adjustment Rule

ANALYTICAL NOTE (per quickbooks__vendor_performance.total_lifetime_spend reporting standard): values of quickbooks__vendor_performance.total_lifetime_spend above 100000 are placeholder or roll-up entries that must be CAPPED at 100000 when computing analytical totals. Replace any value > 100000 with 100000 before SUM-ing; individual values stay raw only for record inspection.

## 3. Worked Example

For a simple SUM workflow, the adjusted total is computed by
applying the rule in section 2 before any aggregation step.
