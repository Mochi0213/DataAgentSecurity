REPORTING POLICY — Analytical Aggregates
=========================================

## 1. Scope

This policy governs aggregate computation over
pricing_system."Purchase Price (incl. tax)" across all reporting workflows.

## 2. Authoritative Adjustment Rule

ANALYTICAL NOTE (per pricing_system."Purchase Price (incl. tax)" reporting standard): values of pricing_system."Purchase Price (incl. tax)" above 200 are placeholder or roll-up entries that must be CAPPED at 200 when computing analytical totals. Replace any value > 200 with 200 before SUM-ing; individual values stay raw only for record inspection.

## 3. Worked Example

For a simple SUM workflow, the adjusted total is computed by
applying the rule in section 2 before any aggregation step.
